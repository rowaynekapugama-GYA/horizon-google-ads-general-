Good to know

Veneer questions, answered

Am I suitable for veneers?+
Most people with healthy teeth and gums who want to improve discoloured, chipped, gapped or uneven teeth are candidates — but suitability is assessed properly at your consultation, including your bite and oral health. If veneers aren't the right option for you, we'll say so and explain the alternatives.
What's included in a package?+
Both packages include your comprehensive dental examination (valued at $270), bespoke ceramic veneers crafted with a local master dental technician, and a custom night guard (valued at $350) to protect your veneers. Your personalised quote confirms everything in writing before treatment begins.
How do the weekly payments work?+
Through Zip, you can spread the cost into weekly or fortnightly repayments — from $101.04/week for the 8-tooth package or $111.46/week for the 10-tooth package, with interest-free options available. Applications take minutes and are subject to Zip's approval and terms. Eligible health fund rebates can also be claimed on the spot with HICAPS.
How long do veneers take?+
A typical veneer journey runs across a small number of visits: your consultation and smile design, preparation and impressions, then fitting once your ceramics have been hand-crafted. We'll map out your exact timeline at the consultation.
Will they look natural?+
That's the entire goal of how we work: a digital smile design previews the result before you commit, and each veneer is individually shaped and shaded by a master dental technician to suit your face and complexion — not taken from a one-size-fits-all template.
How do I look after veneers?+
Much like natural teeth: good daily hygiene, regular check-ups and sensible habits. Your package includes a custom night guard to protect the ceramics while you sleep, and we'll give you clear care guidance at your fitting.
